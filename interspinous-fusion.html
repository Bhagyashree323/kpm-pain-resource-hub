Minimally Invasive Interspinous-Interlaminar Fusion Guide

Direct Clinical Summary: Minimally invasive interspinous-interlaminar fusion stabilizes a painful lumbar segment through a small posterior incision without pedicle screws, designed for lumbar spinal stenosis with mild instability or grade 1 spondylolisthesis.

3-Step Patient Evaluation Checklist

  1. Document walking tolerance, neurogenic claudication, and relief when sitting.
  2. Obtain lumbar MRI and flexion-extension X-rays to assess segmental motion.
  3. Verify insurance prior-authorization criteria and recovery restrictions.
