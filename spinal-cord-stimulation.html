Spinal Cord Stimulation (SCS) Trial & Therapy Guide

Direct Clinical Summary: Spinal cord stimulation delivers mild electrical pulses to the spinal cord to reduce chronic neuropathic pain, post-laminectomy syndrome, CRPS, and painful diabetic neuropathy. A 5-to-7-day temporary lead trial verifies at least 50% pain reduction before permanent implant.

3-Step Patient Evaluation Checklist

  1. List prior medicines, therapy, injections, and spine surgeries with outcomes.
  2. Complete medical clearance, imaging review, and psychological screening.
  3. Track pain scores and daily function during the 5-to-7-day temporary lead trial.
