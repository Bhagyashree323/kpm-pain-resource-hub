Peripheral & Diabetic Neuropathy Treatment Guide

Direct Clinical Summary: Peripheral neuropathy causes burning, tingling, or numbness in the feet and hands. Care combines treating the cause (glucose control, B12 repletion), nerve-targeting medicines (duloxetine, pregabalin, gabapentin), physical therapy, and 10 kHz spinal cord stimulation for refractory diabetic neuropathy.

3-Step Patient Evaluation Checklist

  1. Record symptom onset, sock-and-glove pattern, and night-time flares.
  2. Complete A1c/fasting glucose, vitamin B12, thyroid, and kidney labs.
  3. Ask a pain specialist about nerve blocks or a 10 kHz SCS trial if medicines fall short.
