Non-Surgical Shoulder Pain Care Guide

Direct Clinical Summary: Chronic shoulder pain often stems from rotator cuff tendinopathy, bursitis, frozen shoulder, or arthritis. Ultrasound-guided injections, hydrodilatation, physical therapy, and suprascapular nerve blocks relieve pain without surgery.

3-Step Patient Evaluation Checklist

  1. Note overhead reach pain, night pain, stiffness, and any neck/arm tingling.
  2. Use X-ray, ultrasound, or MRI to distinguish cuff, capsular, and cervical sources.
  3. Pair image-guided injections or suprascapular nerve blocks with structured therapy.
