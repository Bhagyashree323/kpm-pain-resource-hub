Basivertebral Nerve Ablation (Intracept) Guide

Direct Clinical Summary: Basivertebral nerve ablation (Intracept) is an outpatient fluoroscopy-guided procedure for adults with chronic low back pain lasting six months or more and Type 1 or Type 2 Modic endplate changes on MRI.

3-Step Patient Evaluation Checklist

  1. Document at least 6 months of conservative care (therapy, medicines, injections).
  2. Confirm Type 1 or Type 2 Modic changes at L3–S1 on lumbar MRI.
  3. Rule out facet, SI joint, and radicular sources before scheduling BVNA.
